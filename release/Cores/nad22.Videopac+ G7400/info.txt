Videopac+ G7400 / Odyssey2 for Analogue Pocket

The G7400 mode is selected by default. Use the core menu to switch to Videopac G7000 (PAL) or Odyssey2 (NTSC).

Required BIOS and character ROM data files are listed in data.json. These files are not included; place the matching files in `Assets/odyssey2/nad22.Videopac+ G7400/` on the Pocket SD card before launching the core.

Cartridges up to 16 KiB are supported. Current port limitations: The Voice and USB keyboard input are not implemented.
